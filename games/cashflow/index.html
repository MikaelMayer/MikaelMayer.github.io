How to play

You are trying to build passive income — money that arrives whether or not you go to work — until it is greater than your total monthly expenses. The moment it is, you are out of the Rat Race. Do it in as few months as you can.

A salary is not passive income. Neither is a lump sum from selling something. Only rent, dividends, interest and business profit count, which is why selling a property for a good price can still move you backwards.

Roll, move, and resolve the square you land on. Tap any square on the board to see what it does. You may always look at a deal and then decline it, and you may always refuse a purchase.

Once your passive income passes your expenses you leave the Rat Race for the Fast Track, where you win by landing on your own dream and buying it, or by doubling the monthly income you arrived with.

You can take a loan in $1,000 blocks at $100 a month each — 120% a year, interest only. That interest is an expense like any other, so every loan raises the bar you are trying to clear. Nothing is ever borrowed on your behalf: taking a loan is always your own deliberate choice, and you repay it from the Liabilities list.

Keyboard: r rolls, u undoes. Your game saves itself in this browser as you play.

Privacy

This game runs entirely in your browser. It makes no network requests, has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies, and loads no fonts, scripts or images from any other site.

Three things are kept on this device, and only on this device:

  • The game you are playing, saved as you go, so closing the tab does not lose it.
  • Your language choice.
  • Which professions you have got out of the Rat Race with.

None of it identifies you, and none of it is sent anywhere. Two things leave your browser only when you ask them to: Export writes a file where you choose to put it, and a share link carries the seed, profession and dream — nothing else.

The page itself is served by a web host, which like every web host records the IP addresses that request it. That happens before this game runs and is outside its control.
