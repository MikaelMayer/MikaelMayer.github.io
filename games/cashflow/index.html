How to play

You are trying to build passive income — money that arrives whether or not you go to work — until it is greater than your total monthly expenses. The moment it is, you are out of the Rat Race. Do it in as few months as you can.

A salary is not passive income. Neither is a lump sum from selling something. Only rent, dividends, interest and business profit count, which is why selling a property for a good price can still move you backwards.

Roll, move, and resolve the square you land on. Tap any square on the board to see what it does. You may always look at a deal and then decline it, and you may always refuse a luxury.

You can take a loan in $1,000 blocks at $100 a month each — 120% a year, interest only. That interest is an expense like any other, so every loan raises the bar you are trying to clear. Nothing is ever borrowed on your behalf: taking a loan is always your own deliberate choice, and you repay it from the Liabilities list.

Keyboard: r rolls, u undoes. Your game saves itself in this browser as you play.
